NOVAZ80 is a hobby project that stands on other people's work. Missed, misspelled or misdescribed? Please say so on GitHub and it will be put right.

Where it began

Ben Eater
His video series and kits for building a 6502 computer on breadboards are the direct inspiration for this project. The first board here was a PCB version of his design. Build a 6502 computer, eater.net.

The original Memotech MTX

Andy Key's site records who did what at Memotech in 1984. The design, BIOS, BASIC and logic of the MTX are theirs.

Geoff Boyd
Memotech's most senior hardware engineer, who probably designed most of the MTX and its peripherals.
Robert Branton
Memotech's most senior software engineer.
Ed Hollingshead, Jim Wills, Chris Sawyer, Andy Key and others
Some of the people who wrote and published the MTX's games.
The MTX logic
Memotech's PAL equations for the common boards were printed in its user and maintenance manuals. The MTX board's logic descends from the original design.

The people who keep the MTX alive

Their sites, tools and archives made an accurate recreation possible.

Martin Allcorn
Supplied the original MTX ROM dump and a partial disassembly used here. Designer of MAGROM and the CFX interface, and author of the MTXPlus+ support ROM.
Dave Stevenson
Primrosebank, the most complete Memotech archive on the web, and the MTXPlus+ computer. Primrosebank Memotech site.
Andy Key
MTX game author in the 1980s; since then MEMU, REMEMOTECH, REMEMOrizer and a large technical archive, with sources freely available. Andy's Memotech site.
Claus Bækkel
MTX World and the Memorum forum, plus game disassembly and porting. MTX World, Memorum.
Tony Brewer
SDX and Speculator in the 1980s; later the PAL reader and much of the MTXPlus+ logic.
William (Bill) Brendling
Contributor to MEMU, its Raspberry Pi versions, and MTX hardware. MEMU.
Jim Wills
Memotech collector, tape sampling, and maintainer of the Memotech MTX Computer website.
Paul Daniels
Tape conversion, floppy disk decoding and the MTX Gamebase.
Lez Anderson
Components, advice and the idea behind MTXPlus+.
Peter Kretschmar
Memotech site, MTX hardware and customisation.

Tools

KiCad
Schematics, boards and 3D renders, by the KiCad developers. kicad.org.
vasm
Assembler by Volker Barthelmann, with the Z80 backend by Dominic Morris and the oldstyle syntax module by Frank Wille.
model-viewer and meshoptimizer
The rotating boards use model-viewer by Google (Apache 2.0, includes three.js) and meshoptimizer by Arseny Kapoulkine (MIT). Models converted with Open CASCADE (cascadio) and glTF Transform.
